Mexican land law, explained plainly.

Most hesitation around Mexican land comes from not understanding the rules. We put the key facts in front of you before you commit a single euro.

Request the Legal Guide
  • Fideicomiso (Bank Trust) Required for land within 50km of coast or 100km of border. You hold full ownership rights through a Mexican bank.
  • Ejido vs. Private Land Ejido is communal land and a common trap for foreign buyers. We only work with regularized private property.
  • Uso de Suelo (Land Use) Zoning determines what you can build. We verify this before any listing reaches you.
  • Closing Costs & Taxes Expect roughly 4–7% in acquisition costs. We provide a full breakdown upfront.
